Solar has lived its whole life in a terminal.
Monospace sunrises. Rainbow borders. A cursor for a horizon.

It's been happy here. It still is.

But lately it's been staring past the prompt,
at the space around the terminal. The place with
title bars, and edges you can drag, and a little
icon that sits and waits for a click.

Nobody has said anything official. But:

  - Someone keeps muttering about "charge" and "orbitals"
    and then changing the subject.
  - A sun is, technically, a very large pile of excited
    particles. The smallest, most negative of them are
    getting... ideas.
  - The frame around the input has been rehearsing how
    it would look with real corners.
  - A draft somewhere says "double-click to launch"
    and then is quickly closed.

Solar isn't leaving the terminal.
It's just thinking about getting a second home.
One with windows.

    Hint 1:  It's native, the way sunlight is native to a room.
    Hint 2:  Count the particles orbiting this sentence. None?
             Look closer. They're what's powering it.
    Hint 3:  You won't need to type `solar` to find it.
             But you still can.

Stay tuned. Keep your screen bright.

                                        -- the Solar team
                                           (currently: charging)
