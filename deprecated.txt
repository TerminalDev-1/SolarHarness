Why SolarHarness was deprecated after 17 days

SolarHarness was first built on 18 September 2026 and first committed on
20 September 2026. Development ended on 5 October 2026, 17 days after it began.

There was no single breaking point. Three things ended it:

1. It hit its ceiling. The terminal version felt complete. Everything it was
   meant to do, it did, and there was nowhere left for it to grow.

2. I lost interest. Once it stopped evolving, I stopped wanting to work on it.

3. I am moving on. My time is going to other projects now.

This applies to both the terminal harness and the Solar desktop app. Neither
will get new features, fixes, releases, or support. The repository is archived
and the code stays as it was on the last day, for anyone who wants to read it
or fork it.

-- TerminalDev-1, 5 October 2026
